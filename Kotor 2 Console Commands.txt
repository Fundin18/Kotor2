====================================================================
KOTOR 2 CONSOLE COMMANDS & ITEM REFERENCE
====================================================================
Item mappings checked against installed Steam game resources, 2026-10-21.
Includes the installed TSLRCM global item override layer.
Selected item categories and destinations; not every game resource.
Resource mappings verified; commands have not been playtested.

ENABLE AND USE THE CONSOLE
In swkotor2.ini, add EnableCheats=1 under [Game Options].
Press the console key (` or ~ on a US keyboard); it is invisible.
Type the command and press Enter. Do not type placeholder brackets.
Example: giveitem w_blaste_02 gives an Ion Blaster.
For commands below, replace [value] or [amount] with a number.

====================================================================
CORE ENGINE & UTILITY COMMANDS
====================================================================
heal                      - Restore health and Force points
invulnerability           - Toggle invulnerability
infiniteuses              - Toggle unlimited uses for limited-use items
turbo                     - Toggle faster character movement
revealmap                 - Reveal the current area map
bright                    - Toggle full brightness
whereami                  - Output coordinates to the invisible console
restartminigame           - Restart the current minigame
